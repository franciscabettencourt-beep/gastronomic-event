Coral - 4 September

Atlântico

The sea arrives before the wine. At Coral, João Viegas cooks four hands with Diogo Pereira, the guest and the head chef at the same pass. Shellfish opened to order, rice that will not be hurried, salt and acidity in balance. A table guided by what the tide left that morning.

Chefs

  • Diogo Pereira

    At Vilalara every day of the year, Diogo Pereira brings the Atlantic to the table. His cuisine celebrates the Algarve through its finest produce, seasonality and a deep connection to the sea, a philosophy recognised by the MICHELIN Guide Portugal 2026.

  • João Viegas

    For one evening, João Viegas brings his own interpretation of the Algarve to Vilalara. With an internationally shaped career and a MICHELIN Guide Portugal 2026 recommendation, his cuisine brings together local ingredients, contemporary technique and a deep sense of place.

Culinary fusion

At Coral Éden do Mar the Atlantic sets the menu, and restraint does the rest. One evening only, on 4 September.

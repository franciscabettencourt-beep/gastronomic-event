Simplicity as a destination, not a starting point. Stefano Bula joins our kitchen at the Trattoria, with pasta made by hand, olive oil and ripe tomato. Few ingredients, when they are the right ones. You stay at the table longer than you planned.

    Stefano Bula brings the precision of Italian fine dining to the Mediterranean. Head Chef of Gusto by Heinz Beck, a MICHELIN-starred restaurant, his cuisine balances technique, lightness and exceptional seasonal produce.
